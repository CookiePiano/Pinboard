**Sticky notes for your desktop. A place for the things you want to remember.**

Keep today's plans, shopping lists and little ideas where you can see them.
Pinboard is free to use. You do not need an account or an AI connection to take notes.

## Start in three steps

1. Use **Download for Windows** above and install the app.
2. Open **Pinboard** and start typing in a note. Your changes save automatically.
3. Click **＋** at the top of a note to make another one.

## Make yourself at home

- **Write it down** — capture a thought or a to-do and move the note where you need it.
- **Make it yours** — choose a color, transparency and text size.
- **Keep it in view** — pin a note above other windows or to your desktop.
- **Find it again** — use the calendar to open notes by the date you created them.

## Common questions

**Does closing a note delete it?**

The **×** on a note hides it. Right-click Pinboard in the taskbar notification area and choose **Show all notes** to bring it back.

**How do I switch to English?**

Click the gear on a note, then **일반 (General) → 언어 (Language Setting) → English**.

**Can I back up my notes?**

Go to **Settings → Sync → Export** to save notes and settings in a ZIP file.
